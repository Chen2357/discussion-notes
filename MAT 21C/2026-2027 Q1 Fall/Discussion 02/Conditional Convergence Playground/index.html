By adding positive terms when you are under the target and adding negative terms when you are over the target, you are showing how we can rearrange the series to make it converge to any number!

This magic is enabled by the fact that we have an infinite supply of positive terms

1 + ⅓ + ⅕ + ⅐ + ⋯ = ∞

and also an infinite supply of negative terms

−½ − ¼ − ⅙ − ⅛ − ⋯ = −∞

Since both the positive and negative parts diverge on their own, we have enough “oomph” in each direction to steer the partial sums toward any destination we choose.
